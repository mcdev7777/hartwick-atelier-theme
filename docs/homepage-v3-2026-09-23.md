## Her texture

> **Removed 23 September 2026 (Ivan):** the paper effect, its Current / Paper preview
> switch and its theme settings are gone from the theme, along with the tiles and the
> generator scripts. They are in git history (commit 13d41e5) if it is ever wanted back.

Her layer is her paper photograph (1024 × 1536), stretched over the page (1.41× across,
3.28× down), Normal blend, 22%.

`scripts/make-paper-sheet.py` takes that image out of her PDF and turns it into
`assets/ha-paper-sheet.avif` (42 KB) / `.webp` (74 KB):
- It uses the top 1024 × 768 of the image.
- The tile is made seamless (periodic-plus-smooth decomposition), and slow variations in
  tone are removed.
- The theme shows it at her placement (1444 × 2519 per tile).
- It is calibrated so that **22% on the slider has the contrast of her 22% layer**. Measured
  on her rendered PDF: 0.0089; ours: 0.0092.

Her layer also tints everything 22% toward the photograph's grey-beige, which is why her PDF
looks greyer. That tint is deliberately left out, so `#f3f0e7` shows as she asked.

**Theme settings → Paper texture (test) → Texture** chooses between her **paper sheet** (the
default) and the earlier **fine grain** (calibrated to the Nicci K. reference at 40%).
Defaults are now interface 22% and editorial 30%. The site still opens in **Current** mode;
use the Preview switch or `?grain=on` to see it.

## For Aloha: decided one way, and why

1. **Type sizes.** Her artboard was drawn at the earlier, larger sizes: headings 41–43,
   story titles 30, body 19–24. The build keeps her 22 September type table (hero 32,
   H1 26, H2 20, intro 15, body 13), which she asked to be the one system. Tell us if the
   homepage should deviate.
2. **Story titles** are SemiBold Italic in the artboard. They stay Junicode **Light** Italic,
   per her 18 September rule (no bold Junicode).
3. **Card text voice.** She sets it in Junicode. The site's body voice is currently Noto Mono
   (22 September switch, `ha_body_voice`). Justified monospace falls apart in narrow
   columns, so the text is justified only at 1200px and wider.
4. **"Clothing" labels** ("01 / Clothing", "Explore all Clothing") stay **Apparel**, as the
   section is named. Her new paragraph's "clothing" is kept verbatim.
5. **Register line** "Materials, makers and work in progress" stays "…Masters…" (the 21 Sep
   terminology decision).
6. **The Masters list** in the artboard is still the old roles ("Master Weaver · Rajasthan"
   …). It stays the eleven verified techniques; her own §06 rules out the location.
7. **Paper tabs on `#f3f0e7`.** On her white artboard the card tabs stand out. On
   `#f3f0e7` the paper panel `#EEECE2` is only a shade darker, so they read more quietly.
   It's one setting if she wants more contrast.
8. **The texture image** is the paper photograph she supplied. Please confirm it's licensed
   for use on the site.
9. Black-and-white photographs show in colour on phones, where there is no hover (the §07
   behaviour since 19 September).
