# Hartwick Atelier — Proposed copy for every placeholder

Prepared 14 September 2026 · for Aloha's review · **nothing here is on the site yet**

Everything below is a proposal. It replaces the on-screen placeholders in the
unpublished development theme only after you approve it, line by line or page by
page. Where a line cannot be written by us — a Master's name, a care instruction, a
delivery term, a wearer's words — it says so, and stays a marked placeholder.

## How to read this document

Every block carries a tag:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[F]** | Taken from, or built directly on, Christina's *Framework v3* or the Brand Book. Safe to publish once you approve the phrasing. |
| **[S]** | Taken from the naming spreadsheet (*Hartwick_Style_Naming_Conventions*). Product facts only as the sheet states them. |
| **[W]** | Already on the wireframes / your mockups. Kept unless noted. |
| **[P]** | Proposed by us. Brand-voice copy that states no fact we cannot support. Needs your eye. |
| **[A]** | Needs **Aloha's decision** — a choice between readings, or a number/date/location only you have. |
| **[C]** | Needs **Christina** — legal, consent, health claims, or terminology beyond Framework v3. |
| **[✕]** | **Cannot be written by us.** Stays a labelled placeholder until the real thing arrives. |

Voice, throughout, from Framework v3 §04: *poetic not promotional; specific not
vague; transformative not transactional; honest about what most brands hide.*
Without urgency. British spelling. Every "Register" CTA reads **Join The Register**.
