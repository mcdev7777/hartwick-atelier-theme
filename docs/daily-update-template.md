# Daily update to Aloha — template

Six headings, in this order (her request, `audit-and-plan.md` §Reporting).
Send from the same address each day, same subject shape, so the thread stacks.

**Subject:** Hartwick — Daily update, [Day DD Mon]

---

Aloha,

[One line: what period this covers.]

**Completed**
- [Max 6 bullets. Outcome first, mechanism second. Say what it means for her,
  not what you typed.]

[Standing reassurance line: nothing published, live theme untouched.]

**In progress**
[2–3 sentences. What is on the bench right now and what it unblocks.]

**Hours**
[X] used · [Y] of 40 remaining. [One clause if the burn rate changed.]

**Next priority**
[The single next block of work, and why it is next.]

**Decisions I need**
1. [Blocking today. Question + the options + your recommendation.]
2. [Max 3. Anything else goes under scope watch or waits a day.]

**Scope / 10 September watch**
- [Risk → one-line impact → your recommendation. Carry items forward verbatim
  until answered, so the record shows how long each has been open.]

Ivan

---

## Rules for filling it in

- Never bury a scope risk inside "Completed". It goes in the last section or it
  did not get raised.
- Decisions: three maximum. More than three and none get answered.
- Every open item is repeated daily, unchanged, until she answers. That is the
  paper trail for the 40 hrs and the 10 Sep date.
- No invented content, no invented certainty. `[pending]` stays visible.
- Hours are billing. State them even on a light day.
